Data Engineer

Organisation/Entity:  Transport For NSW
Job category:  Projects
Job location: 

Macquarie Park, NSW, AU, 2113

Job reference number:  107904
Employment type:  Permanent Full-Time
Salary range:  $137551.0-$154055.0

 

 

You crave a career that pushes you, where you'll meet challenges head on and you're guaranteed to grow. 

 

You bring structure, mitigation and education to our organisation. Our records and datasets are vast and varied. This is your opportunity to safeguard critical infrastructure, order and protect sensitive data, and defend against emerging cyber threats. You'll be part of an expert team who protect against thousands of threats every day. 

 

 

In this role, you'll:


This role collaborates closely with stakeholders across technology, business, and analytics teams to enable consistent, scalable, and modern data practices. You will design, build, and maintain scalable data pipelines and integration solutions to support enterprise reporting, analytics, and operational data. You will develop and optimise data models, data marts, and curated datasets. 

 


For more information on this position and business unit, view the role description and information pack.

 

 

About you


You have qualifications in Information Technology, Computer Science, Data Management. You have experience designing, building, and maintaining ETL/ELT data pipelines and data integration processes within enterprise data platforms or cloud data environments. You have strong technical skills in SQL, data warehousing concepts, data modelling.

 

 

Who we are


Transport for NSW provides a safe, integrated, and efficient transport system. We connect people, communities and industry every day.
