Re: Data Engineer — Job Reference 107904

Dear Hiring Manager,

I have spent the past two years building data pipelines and integration solutions inside NSW's most complex health data environments. The fit with this role at Transport for NSW was immediate — scalable pipelines, curated datasets, stakeholders across technology and business — that is the work I do every day, and I want to do it at a larger scale.

I am currently a System Integration Engineer at eHealth NSW, delivering large-scale data migration and ETL pipeline development within one of NSW's most complex enterprise data environments. My core technical contribution has been designing and automating Snowflake-based data pipelines — using UDFs, stored procedures, tasks, and dynamic tables — to transform raw clinical source data into precisely structured formats for ingestion into Epic. Before that, at NSWHP, I managed Azure cloud infrastructure including Kubernetes clusters, SQL servers, and Service Bus integrations, while simultaneously running ETL workstreams that fed downstream clinical reporting. Both roles required me to work closely with clinical system owners, project managers, and engineering teams — translating complex data problems into solutions that all three audiences could trust.

My technical foundation aligns closely with what this role requires: strong SQL and Python for pipeline development and data transformation, hands-on experience with Snowflake as an enterprise cloud data platform, ETL/ELT design across structured and semi-structured data formats, and CI/CD practices to keep pipelines deployable and maintainable. I hold a Master of Data Science from the University of Adelaide, which gives me the analytical foundation to not just build pipelines but to reason about data quality, modelling trade-offs, and the fitness of data for its intended use.

One thing I have learned from working in high-stakes data environments is that technical quality alone is not enough — how you communicate about data, to the right people at the right level of detail, is just as important as how you build it. I have experience translating data engineering decisions for non-technical stakeholders, managing competing priorities across delivery teams, and driving clarity in environments where ownership and requirements are often ambiguous. I bring that same discipline — breaking complex problems into clearly bounded, independently solvable pieces — to both the technical and the human side of data engineering.

I would be genuinely excited to bring this experience to Transport for NSW and contribute to building the data foundations that keep NSW moving.

Sincerely,

Tom Tran
